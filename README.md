# Alpha Infra — Finance & AI concept

A responsive, single-page concept prototype for a potential finance-focused AI practice within Alpha Infra. It specializes the existing independent AI consulting and custom-build positioning for finance, accounting and the Office of the CFO. Prepared as a discussion artifact for a possible joint venture.

The sample dashboard and workflow examples are illustrative, not live client work. Founder backgrounds are based on supplied resume information and public Redwood Trust disclosures linked in the site. David’s CPA is labeled inactive. The prototype makes no client or guaranteed outcome claims.

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. This is a dependency-free static page.

## Concept direction

- Finance AI opportunity mapping and roadmaps
- Close, AP, FP&A and reporting workflow pilots
- Controls, human review, traceable evidence and team enablement

Brand and offer remain open for discussion, including a separate venture identity.
